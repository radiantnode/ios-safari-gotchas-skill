Swipe up to collapse the toolbar, rotate, or switch the tab layout in Settings › Apps › Safari. The numbers follow.

Reading the bottom edge

  • Stripes to the screen's last row: Safari draws the page under its toolbar. How far below the window it reaches depends on the tab layout, not the phone.
  • A flat magenta band with the stripes cut off above it: you are seeing body. Here that only happens past the end of the page. On your own site, a fixed or sticky element sized to the viewport causes it on every screen.
  • Magenta behind the clock: Safari sampled body's colour for the status bar when the page loaded, and keeps it.

The page ends just below

Pull past the last stripe and the rubber band shows body: magenta. Safari re-reads body's colour live for the strip under the toolbar, but reads it only once for the status bar.
